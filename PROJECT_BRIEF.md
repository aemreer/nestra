# PROJECT_BRIEF.md — NESTRA

## 1. Product
**NESTRA** is a fictional premium Turkish brand that designs and sells modern modular living spaces.

It is broader than a tiny-house brand. Tiny homes can exist within the category, but the brand should communicate contemporary modular architecture across different sizes and lifestyles.

NESTRA is a manufacturer/sales brand, not:
- a rental platform
- an Airbnb/Sahibinden clone
- a luxury villa developer
- a generic construction company

Primary commercial actions:
- **Modelleri Keşfet**
- **Teklif Al**
- **Satış Danışmanıyla Görüş** when contextually appropriate

## 2. Brand Character
NESTRA should feel:
- contemporary
- calm
- architectural
- natural
- premium without ostentation
- editorial rather than corporate
- warm rather than sterile
- credible and buildable

Core architectural DNA:
- contemporary Scandinavian + Japanese minimalism
- realistic/buildable modular architecture
- natural materials
- refined metal details
- thin dark window frames
- large glazing
- warm natural interiors
- Turkish natural landscapes
- restrained premium character

The environment can differ by model. The products should not look as though they all sit on the same development site.

Avoid visual excess, obvious CGI aesthetics, and luxury-villa clichés. Even at the flagship level, landscaping must remain controlled and architectural rather than becoming resort-like.

## 3. Product Family — V2 Locked

### NESTRA One
- Role: entry model
- Area: 38 m²
- Plan: 1+1
- Storeys: one storey
- Character: compact but premium; small must not mean cheap or simplistic
- Silhouette: horizontal and calm
- Facade: predominantly light/mid-tone natural wood
- Details: anthracite metal and thin black frames
- Glass: broad glazed surfaces
- Landscape: controlled natural woodland
- Outdoor living: small but refined veranda/deck
- Avoid: container or ordinary tiny-house aesthetics
- Keyword: **Sadelik**

### NESTRA Loft
- Role: vertically expressive compact model
- Ground floor: approximately 40 m²
- Loft: approximately 14 m²
- Total usable area: approximately 54 m²
- Plan: 1+1 with a real loft
- Interior volume: true double-height space
- Massing: the loft volume must be legible from the exterior; more vertical and sculptural than One
- Roof: strong single-direction rising roof
- Exterior: dark charcoal/black surfaces
- Interior and soffits: warm natural wood
- Main facade: large double-height glazing
- Outdoor living: stronger deck
- Landscape: dramatic forest setting with vertical surroundings
- Keyword: **Yükseklik**

The loft must remain a real, usable mezzanine/loft. Do not reinterpret it as merely a taller One.

### NESTRA Family
- Role: family-oriented modular model
- Area: approximately 76 m²
- Plan: 2+1
- Storeys: one storey
- Household: permanent living for 3–4 people
- Form: two or more connected/offset volumes; not an enlarged One
- Living: central living room + open kitchen
- Private spaces: more controlled openings
- Outdoor living: strong veranda/outdoor dining relationship
- Materials: predominantly warm natural wood with dark metal details
- Landscape: more developed, habitable and settled natural landscape
- Character: social, warm and established
- Keyword: **Birliktelik**

### NESTRA Horizon
- Role: NESTRA's flagship model
- Area: approximately 132 m²
- Plan: 3+1
- Storeys: predominantly one storey with a real, usable partial second storey
- Upper volume: a genuine living volume sized for uses such as a master suite, private room or workspace; not a decorative pavilion or thin band
- Form: a very strong horizontal main mass; not merely an enlarged Family
- Roof: broad, elegant overhangs
- Glass: panoramic glazed surfaces
- Outdoor living: strong indoor-outdoor relationship
- Palette: light mineral, warm off-white and light stone tones with natural wood
- Details: thin dark metal or black frames
- Landscape: the most developed landscape in the product family; palms and a controlled water element/reflection pool may be used
- Character: more open and refined than the other models
- Premium principle: flagship quality should come primarily from proportion, openness, materials, landscape and architectural quality rather than size alone
- Avoid: resort and ultra-luxury villa clichés
- Keyword: **Ufuk**

### Product Family Design Principle

The four models are not S/M/L/XL versions of one structure. Each model may have a different:
- silhouette
- massing composition
- roof character
- landscape approach
- dominant material and colour identity

Family resemblance does not come from repeating the exact same architectural form. Shared NESTRA DNA comes from:
- high-quality contemporary architecture
- realistic and buildable proportions
- natural materials
- refined metal details
- broad glazed surfaces
- a strong indoor-outdoor relationship
- integration with nature
- controlled premium character
- high-quality architectural photography

### Landscape Principle

Landscape is part of each model's architectural design, not merely a natural backdrop behind the building. Landscape and outdoor living may become more developed as the model level rises:
- **One:** simpler, natural woodland landscape
- **Loft:** more dramatic forest, stronger deck and vertical surroundings
- **Family:** more social and established veranda/garden relationship
- **Horizon:** the most refined landscape and broader outdoor living; palms and controlled water elements may be used

No model should drift into a resort, hotel, suburban villa development or excessively sterile lawn aesthetic.

## 4. Visual Identity — V1

### Palette
These are V1 values and can be deliberately tuned after testing against real imagery.

- Warm Ivory / main background: `#F3F0E9`
- Soft White / surface: `#FAF9F6`
- Charcoal / main text and dark sections: `#20211F`
- Warm Gray / secondary text: `#74736D`
- Stone / natural secondary surface: `#D8D0C2`
- Olive / restrained accent: `#69705A`

Do not overuse olive. The primary visual identity should come from warm neutrals, charcoal, typography, whitespace, and architectural imagery.

### Typography
V1:
- Editorial/display headings: **Instrument Serif**
- UI, navigation, body, technical information: **Instrument Sans**

Avoid generic startup typography styling and excessive bold weights.

### Geometry
- architectural and relatively square
- imagery can be square-cornered or use very small radius
- cards: low radius, roughly 2–8 px character
- buttons: restrained radius
- admin can use slightly softer geometry than the public site
- avoid ubiquitous `rounded-2xl`

### Logo
Typography-led **NESTRA** wordmark.

Do not generate an arbitrary AI logo or symbol. A separate symbol can be explored later only if there is a real need.

## 9. Server / Client Strategy
Prefer Server Components for content-oriented public UI and data fetching.

Use Client Components only where needed, such as:
- interactive gallery
- mobile navigation
- form interaction
- client-side state
- browser APIs
- selected Motion interactions

Do not make whole pages Client Components merely because one child needs interaction.

## 11. Image Assets and Continuity

The current canonical production exterior image for each model is:

```text
public/images/models/one/hero.png
public/images/models/loft/hero.png
public/images/models/family/hero.png
public/images/models/horizon/hero.png
```

These images define each model's canonical architecture. Future exterior angles, rear elevations, veranda/outdoor living imagery, interiors, living rooms, kitchens, bedrooms, the Loft upper level, the Horizon upper level and floor plans must remain consistent with the relevant canonical design.

Do not create different architectural designs for the same model. Materials, window arrangement, roof form, massing organization and overall architectural identity must remain consistent.

Do not fabricate additional imagery. Additional model angles/interiors will be generated only when the real UI requires them.

## 12. Scope Discipline
V1 intentionally excludes:
- payments
- customer accounts
- favorites
- rental booking
- blog
- fake reviews/testimonials
- excessive 3D
- unnecessary marketing pages

The goal is depth and polish, not feature count.

## 13. Copy Status
Homepage headlines selected above are **V1**, not immutable final copy.

When the real design is visible, review the copy in context. It is acceptable to propose changes, but do not silently rewrite the agreed brand voice.

## 14. Portfolio Goal
The finished project should demonstrate more than a landing page:
- strong frontend/UI craft
- Next.js architecture
- TypeScript
- responsive design
- tasteful animation
- real data integration
- forms and validation
- authentication
- CRUD
- admin workflows
